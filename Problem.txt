(Problem Statement: CAMPUSLINK - AI Powered
Campus-to-Corporate Placement Management &
Analytics Platform
Domain: EdTech + Artificial Intelligence / Placement & Career Services Automation
Background
College and university placement cells are responsible for managing thousands of students,
hundreds of recruiters and dozens of overlapping placement drives every academic season.
Most placement cells still rely on scattered spreadsheets, email chains, WhatsApp groups
and manual shortlisting to run this process, resulting in duplicated effort, missed deadlines
and inconsistent communication.
Students often have no single readiness profile, skill-gap analysis or personalised guidance
on which recruiters or roles they best fit, while recruiters struggle to receive well-matched,
pre-screened candidate pools.
Placement drives frequently clash in scheduling, offer letters and joining statuses are tracked
manually, and placement cells have little to no analytics on which branches, skills or
preparation efforts actually convert into offers.
There is therefore a need for an AI-powered, end-to-end Campus-to-Corporate Placement
Management System that can digitise, automate and intelligently optimise the entire
placement lifecycle.
Challenge
Design and develop an AI-powered Campus-to-Corporate Placement Management
System capable of managing student readiness, matching students to recruiter
requirements, scheduling placement drives and generating actionable placement
analytics in near real time.
The proposed system should use technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine
Learning, Natural Language Processing, recommendation systems or Generative AI to
analyse student profiles, recruiter requirements and historical placement data to streamline
the placement process.
The system should not merely digitise records. It should also provide:
● Student readiness and employability scoring
● Skill-gap analysis against target job roles
● Recruiter-student matching and ranking
● Conflict-free drive scheduling
● Offer, documentation and joining-status tracking
● Predictive analytics on placement outcomes
Key Placement Management Areas
Teams may address a combination of the following:
1. Student Readiness Profiling
Build a comprehensive, continuously updated profile for every student covering:
● Resume and academic records
● Certifications and projects
● Aptitude and mock-interview scores
● Communication and soft-skill assessments
● Branch, CGPA and backlog history
The system should identify indicators such as skill gaps, low readiness scores and areas
needing improvement before a student is put forward to a recruiter.
2. Recruiter Requirement Matching
Parse recruiter job descriptions and eligibility criteria, then identify or assess the best-fit
candidates by:
● Skill and technology-stack matching
● Branch and CGPA eligibility filtering
● Certification and project relevance
● Past interview performance
The system may generate a fit/match score and highlight the reasons a student was or was
not shortlisted.
3. Drive Scheduling & Conflict Management
Detect and resolve scheduling issues such as:
● Overlapping company drives on the same date/slot
● Venue and resource double-booking
● Students shortlisted for multiple simultaneous drives
● Interview-panel and infrastructure availability
4. Offer & Documentation Tracking
Track the complete post-selection lifecycle, including:
● Offer letters and CTC details
● Joining bonds, documentation and verification status
● PPOs (pre-placement offers) and internship conversions
● Offer acceptance, deferral or withdrawal
5. Analytics & Predictive Insights
Generate insights such as:
● Branch-wise and skill-wise placement conversion rates
● Salary and package trends across recruiters ● Students at risk of remaining unplaced ● Recruiter engagement and repeat-hiring patterns 6. Communication & Notification Automation Replace manual email/WhatsApp coordination with automated, targeted notifications for: ● Shortlisting and interview schedules ● Document submission deadlines ● Offer status updates ● Drive announcements and eligibility criteria Expected Solution The proposed prototype should preferably contain the following modules: A. Multi-Source Data Integration Engine The system may integrate and analyse: ● Student academic and resume data ● Recruiter job descriptions and eligibility criteria ● Placement drive calendars ● Historical placement records ● Assessment and mock-interview results B. AI-Based Matching & Scoring Engine Participants may use: ● Machine Learning ● Natural Language Processing ● Recommendation Systems ● Large Language Models ● Predictive Analytics ● Rule + AI Hybrid Models C. Readiness & Fit Scoring Every student-recruiter pairing should be assigned an appropriate readiness/fit level, for example: Not Ready → Developing → Ready → Highly Employable The system should explain the major factors behind the score. D. Explainable Recommendations Instead of providing only: “Not Shortlisted” the system should provide an explanation such as: “Below Threshold: The student's CGPA meets the eligibility criteria, but the required skill set shows a gap in cloud technologies and the mock-interview score is below the recruiter's expected benchmark.” For recruiter-side recommendations, the system may similarly explain why a candidate pool was ranked in a particular order. E. Intelligent Workflow Automation Depending on the placement stage, the system may recommend or automate: ● Auto-scheduling of drives around detected conflicts ● Auto-shortlisting based on eligibility and fit score ● Personalised preparation recommendations for students ● Automated reminders for pending documentation ● Escalation of at-risk/unplaced students to mentors ● Recruiter follow-up prompts for pending offers F. Placement Command Dashboard Develop an interactive dashboard displaying: ● Total students registered and placement-ready ● Active and upcoming drives ● Offers made, accepted and pending ● Branch-wise and skill-wise conversion rates ● Average and highest package trends ● Students at risk of remaining unplaced ● Recruiter pipeline and engagement status ● Documentation and joining-status summary Innovation Opportunities Participants are encouraged to explore advanced capabilities such as: ● Generative AI-assisted resume and profile building ● AI-powered mock interview and communication-skill analysis ● Chatbot-based FAQ and eligibility assistant for students ● Predictive at-risk-student identification ● Recruiter-student matching using embeddings/vector search ● Explainable AI for shortlisting decisions ● Real-time drive-conflict detection ● Blockchain-based offer-letter verification ● Integration with LinkedIn/job-portal APIs
● Gamified skill-building and preparation recommendations ● Mobile app for real-time alerts and updates ● Multi-campus/multi-cell analytics aggregation Minimum Expected Prototype Each team should demonstrate at least three core placement-management capabilities, of which preferably: 1. One should involve student readiness profiling and skill-gap analysis. 2. One should involve recruiter-student matching and drive scheduling. 3. One should involve analytics/predictive insights and offer tracking. The prototype should demonstrate: Profiling → Matching → Scheduling → Notification → Offer Tracking → Analytics Minimum Deliverables Teams should submit/demonstrate: 1. Working prototype. 2. Student readiness/employability scoring mechanism. 3. Recruiter-student matching for at least three simulated drives. 4. Conflict-aware drive scheduling mechanism. 5. Explainable shortlisting/matching output. 6. Placement monitoring dashboard. 7. Offer and documentation tracking mechanism. 8. System architecture. 9. Details of models/algorithms used. 10. Demonstration using simulated or publicly available placement/recruitment datasets. 11. Accuracy/performance evaluation of matching and scoring. 12. Scalability and deployment approach across multiple campuses. Desired Impact The proposed solution should have the potential to evolve into an intelligent Campus-toCorporate Placement Ecosystem that can be deployed across university and college placement cells. The objective is to create a system that improves placement outcomes for students, simplifies recruiter engagement, and gives placement cells data-driven visibility and control over the entire hiring pipeline.)
